# Fixed-data convergence audit, 2026-10-20
Question: Was the 240-epoch estimator stopped before a practical validation plateau, and can more optimization improve it?
Unit: one seeded optimization continuation; correlated frames are not independent trials. No confidence claims from frame count.
Starting weights: causal_v3_extended/aligned_window_720.pt (240 epochs, 7680 updates). Optimizer moments were not saved; restart AdamW explicitly. Seed 20261020.
Fixed data: physical-label-corrected 29101/29102 training, 29201 validation. Keep 25x82 model, original loss, 448 uniform + 64 transition samples, 32 updates/epoch. No new steady-state data, no blind data in training/selection.
Validation every 10 epochs, fixed subset including complete transition windows. Preserve original six-term ranking and additionally reject any original guarded metric >110% of the starting 240-epoch model. Log fixed validation loss, component errors, magnitude/direction metrics, and training loss.
Learning rate: 3e-6 initially; after 120 epochs without a >=0.2% improvement in eligible best validation score, reduce to 1e-6, then 3e-7. Minimum 160 epochs per stage. After the last reduction, require >=180 epochs and >=180 epochs without a >=0.2% eligible-score improvement. Also require the last two 60-epoch validation windows to show <0.5% improvement in median score and fixed validation loss, <1% improvement in each of the six ranked metrics, and <=3% absolute median drift in score/loss. This is practical validation convergence at the declared resolution, not a proof of a global optimum. Continuing decline in training loss alone does not prevent validation convergence.
No epoch limit is evidence of convergence. A 10000-total-epoch safety stop returns NOT_CONVERGED and preserves resumable state; never label a resource stop converged.
Save resumable optimizer/RNG states every 20 epochs and on every best update. Select only from fixed validation. Freeze checkpoint before fresh MuJoCo paired evaluation on new seeds 29901/29902. Compare against 240-epoch model on identical force plans and GPU; check physical label audits, reset/termination counts, five scenes, 0–15/15–45N, direction tails and transient windows. Accuracy/stability acceptance is separate from convergence; do not replace default deployment if either fails.
